# Brand rules for the website

Summary of the Dutch Cloud Solutions brand guide (as of 31 October 2026), limited to what matters when editing this site. The full guide, tokens, logo files and component previews live in the Dutch Cloud Solutions design system.

## Core lines (use verbatim)

| Line | Text | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tagline | Sharper go-to-market. Safer vendor choices. | Hero, Open Graph image, footer |
| Descriptor | Cloud go-to-market for ISVs. Vendor assessment for the enterprise. | Directly under the tagline |
| Proof line | From Microsoft Marketplace launch to sovereign-ready selection, across Western Europe and the Nordics. | Hero, intro text |
| Method line | Sovereignty by workload, not by vendor. | Method section only, never as the page headline |

- The tagline's first half speaks to ISVs, the second to enterprises. Keep that order.
- Show the two practices side by side as equals (`FOR ISVS`, `FOR ENTERPRISES`). No arrows, funnels or "connecting vendors and buyers" language between them.
- Practice names in full: "ISV Go-to-Market and Customer Success" and "Enterprise Vendor Selection and Assessment".

## Name and logo

- Write "Dutch Cloud Solutions" in full; never "DCS" in visible text. Legal footer: "Dutch Cloud Solutions BV".
- Logo: divider wordmark, DUTCH (ExtraBold) + orange-600 bar + Cloud Solutions (Regular). Navy on light, white on dark; the bar stays orange.
- Clear space equals the cap height of DUTCH. Minimum width 180px.
- Small mark (favicon, avatar): white D and orange bar on a navy tile.

## Voice

- US English. Direct, factual enterprise prose; lead with the answer.
- No em dashes: use colons, commas, parentheses or a new sentence. Ranges as "3 to 4 weeks".
- Avoid: delve, unlock, seamless, game-changing, "in today's fast-paced world", "it's not just X, it's Y", stacked rhetorical triplets. No emoji.
- Capability lists use bold-lead bullets: "**Placement:** which Spectrum tiers the product must support."
- Regulatory and market facts carry an as-of date and a linked source. Proposals (for example CADA) are labeled proposed. Planning numbers are labeled as assumptions. Unshipped product capabilities carry a Roadmap tag.
- Any content that recommends a product Dutch Cloud Solutions resells or is affiliated with must disclose that relationship and name at least one alternative considered.

| blue-600 / primary | #1D5FA8 | #4A8FD9 | Links, primary states, hyperscaler path |
| teal-700 / secondary | #0B7A6B | #3CCBB5 | Eyebrows, sovereign path |
| orange-600 / cta | #E8700A | #F28C28 | One call to action per view; logo bar |
| amber-400 | #F5B700 | #F5B700 | Highlight, dark-mode focus ring |
| Tier 0 to 4 | #1D5FA8 to #0F9D8A | #4A8FD9 to #3CCBB5 | Sovereignty Spectrum ramp only |

- CTA buttons: orange fill with navy text (never white text on orange).
- orange-600, teal-600 and amber-400 are graphic colors on light grounds, not text colors.
- Never use Azure blue #0078D4, EU-flag blue #003399 or purple. Never color tiers red or green.

## Type

Inter Variable (Inter Display optical size for headings) and JetBrains Mono Variable, both self-hosted. No Google Fonts or other font CDNs. Mono is for eyebrows, data, tier codes and code only.

## Signature elements

- **Mono eyebrow labels:** `01 / ASSESS`, `// TIER 2`, `FOR ISVS`. Phases are always Assess, Design, Launch, Operate.
- **Bracket callouts:** one key fact in orange mono brackets, at most two per view.
- **Tier stripe:** 4px five-segment rule at the top of the header and the foot of the page.

## Imagery

Architecture and flow diagrams in brand colors; Dutch landscape duotone (navy to teal) for heroes. No padlocks, flags, clouds with shields, robots, stock handshakes or blue-purple gradients.
