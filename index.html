// Method

Sovereignty by workload, not by vendor.

Most estates need both ends. We place each workload on evidence: a hyperscaler when it needs speed, scale, on-demand capacity, productivity or cost optimization; a sovereign option when it must stay within EU jurisdiction or a data boundary.

// AI activation

Index in place. Serve any assistant.

We bring enterprise knowledge to AI without moving it out of its tier. Content is indexed where it lives and served over the Model Context Protocol (MCP), so your choice of assistant stays open.

  • Model-agnostic: one MCP endpoint for Microsoft 365 Copilot, Gemini, Amazon Q or an EU-hosted model.
  • Placement first: the index stays in the tier the assessment chose for the data.
  • Measured: retrieval quality, cost and adoption measured on one data domain before roll-out.

For enterprises · Selection integrity

Independent by contract, not by claim.

When we help you choose a vendor, these rules are written into the engagement.

  1. 01

    Disclosure: every proposal lists our current vendor clients and reseller agreements.

  2. 02

    Paid by you only: no vendor fee, commission or resale margin on your deal unless you approve it in writing.

  3. 03

    Criteria first: shortlist and proof-of-concept criteria are agreed before any vendor is named.

  4. 04

    Your call on conflicts: if a vendor we work with is shortlisted, you decide whether we continue with disclosure or an independent reviewer scores that vendor.

  5. 05

    One side per deal: we never represent a vendor and advise the buyer in the same procurement.

// Why now · As of 31 October 2026

The rules and the market moved in 2026.

Placement decisions made now carry contract terms, procurement rules and budgets into 2027.

12 Jan 2027

EU Data Act: switching charges end

Cloud providers may no longer charge for switching, including data egress for the switch. Until then, charges are capped at direct cost.

Source: Latham & Watkins

15 Aug 2026

NIS2 in force in the Netherlands

The Cyberbeveiligingswet, the Dutch implementation of NIS2, took effect with new duties of care and incident reporting.

Source: CMS

European sovereign cloud IaaS, 2027

Gartner forecasts European spending rising from $6.9B in 2025 to $23.1B in 2027, overtaking North America.

Source: Gartner, February 2026

CADA assurance levels Proposed

The Commission's Cloud and AI Development Act proposal defines four Union assurance levels for public-sector cloud.

Source: Irish Department of Enterprise
